• The persistence context is the first-level cache and the unit-of-work boundary.
• It tracks managed entities, their snapshots for dirty checking, and pending inserts, updates, and deletes…
• In a typical Spring Boot app it is transaction-scoped: open when the transactional method starts, close…
• Open-session-in-view can stretch it across the whole web request — convenient for lazy reads, dangerous…
