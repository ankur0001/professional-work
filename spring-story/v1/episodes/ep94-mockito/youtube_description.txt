The Spring Story — Episode 94: Mockito

The Spring Story is a structured series that teaches Spring Framework and Spring Boot from first principles through production practice — one clear episode at a time.

In this episode:
• Real billing services are not: they ask a DutyProvider port for customs duty rates before summing the quote.
• In production that port may call a remote duty table.
• In a unit test you do not want that network.
• Mockito creates the stand-in, stubs returns, and verifies interactions while JUnit executes the real subject.
• @ExtendWith(MockitoExtension.class) hooks Mockito into Jupiter.
• @Mock creates fake DutyProvider and TariffRepository.
• @InjectMocks builds TariffService with those mocks — it prefers constructor injection when the class is…
• when(...).thenReturn(...) stubs duty and base.
• …and more
